Healthy Maple Pear French Toast (Vegan, Gluten Free)

French Toast

  • 2 slices bread of choice

  • 1/2 ripe banana

  • 1/2 TBSP ground flax seeds

  • 1/3 cup unsweetened almond milk

  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

  • 1 stevia packet

Maple Pear Compote

  • 1 TBSP maple syrup

  • 1/2 ripe pear

  1. Mash the banana in a bowl. Then add the flax seeds, almond milk, vanilla extract, and stevia. It should be liquid-y. If not, add a little bit more almond milk.

  2. Soak your bread in this mixture for 2 minutes per side.

  3. Spray a large pan with nonstick spray and heat to medium. Add your soaked bread; let cook for 3-4 minutes per side, until lightly brown. Remove and set to the side.

  4. Thinly slice the ripe pear, then add it to the pan with maple syrup. Let cook on a low heat for 2-3 minutes, or until softened. Pour on top of your french toast.

  5. Enjoy! Feel free to add toppings (I used sliced almonds and cocoa almond Square Bars)
