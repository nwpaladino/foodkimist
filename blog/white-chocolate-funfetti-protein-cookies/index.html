White Chocolate Funfetti Protein Cookies

Makes: 7 cookies

  • 1 cup raw oats

  • 2 scoops corfetti cellucor protein powder

  • 1/4 cup baking stevia

  • 1/4 cup unsweetened almond milk

  • 1 whole egg

  • 3 TBSP coconut oil

  • 2 oz sugar free white chocolate chips

  • 1/4 tsp baking soda

  • SPRINKLES

Grind the oats into a flour. Mix it in a bowl with the remaining ingredients! Should be pretty thick! Plop BIG blobs of batter onto a sprayed baking sheet. These cookies are BIGGG and manly . You should have 7 cookies! Bake at 350 F for 8-10 minutes! Dance (required).

1 cookie: 183 cals; 9 g fat, 15 g carbs (2 g fiber), and 10 g protein
