Cherry Coconut Espresso Parfait

Breakfast Recipe

You know me and my parfait love :DDDD This one is puffed millet, frozen cherries, espresso overnight oatmeal, unsweetened shredded coconut, and chocolate brownie coconut butter by Sweet Spreads.Cherry Coconut Espresso Parfait Seves: 1Espresso Overnight Oatmeal

  • 1/3 cup raw old fashioned rolled oats
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 TBSP chia seeds
  • 1/2 tsp instant espresso powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 stevia packets

Other Layers

  • 1/2 cup puffed millet
  • 1/2 cup frozen cherries
  • 2 TBSP unsweetened shredded coconut
  • 1/2 TBSP chocolate brownie coconut butter (Sweet Spreads brand)

Mix all of the oatmeal ingredients in a bowl. Place in the fridge overnight so the oats soak and the chia seeds plump up. The next morning, layer it with the other layer ingredients, and enjoy!
