Cherry Garcia Protein Truffles

Makes: 14 truffles (1 serving = 3 truffles)

  • 2 scoops vanilla protein powder (70 g)

  • 1/4 cup melted coconut oil

  • 1/4 cup coconut flour

  • 2 stevia packets

  • 6 TBSP unsweetened almond milk.

  • 1 cocoa cherry nutritional bar

  • 5-6 oz sugar free chocolate

Mix the protein powder, coconut oil, coconut flour, stevia, and almond milk in a bowl. It should be thick!! Once evenly mixed (it's gotta be thick so muster up strength to mix it), break apart and stir in one cocoa cherry bar. THEN place the mix in the fridge for 15 mins to thicken. Once thickened a wee tad lil bit, remove and roll into 14 balls!! THENNN melt 5-6 oz sugar free chocolate by microwaving it for 1:30-2 minutes. Roll the truffles in your melty meltayyy chocolate. Refrigerate another 5-10 mins to solidify the chocolate then EEEEEEEAT.
