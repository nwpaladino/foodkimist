Vegan Funfetti Cake Bars (gluten free)

Makes: 12 cake bars

  • 1.5 cups rolled oats

  • 1 TBSP ground flax

  • 1 serving Nuzest (25 g) plant based protein

  • 1/4 cup baking stevia

  • 2 tsp baking powder

  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk

  • 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce

  • 1/2 cup cashew butter (I use Crazy Richard’s brand!)

  • 2-3 drops princess cake baking emulsion (Optional - I get it on Amazon)

  • Sprinkles!

  1. Grind the oats into a flour. Then in a medium bowl, mix together the ground oats and the remaining ingredients. It'll be pretty thick .

  2. Spray a 9×9 baking dish with nonstick spray and pour your batter in. Bake at 350 F for 22-25 mins.

  3. Let cool 10 minutes then cut into 12 pieces. Enjoy!
