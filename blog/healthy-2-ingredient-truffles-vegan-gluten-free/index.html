Healthy 2 Ingredient Truffles (vegan, gluten free)

Makes: 10 truffles (serves 2)

  • 1 pumpkin spice Lenny & Larry cookie

  • 1/4 cup peanut flour (PB2, or any similar brand)

  • 1/4 cup water

  • sprinkles, for topping

  • unsweetened cocoa, for topping

  1. Mash the cookie into a bowl. It should crumble easily.

  2. Add the peanut flour and water. Continue to mash until a thick batter forms. If, after mixing for 3-5 minutes, the batter still doesn't 'bind,' add 1 TBSP more of water.

  3. Roll the batter into 10 truffles. Feel free to roll/decorate the truffles with sprinkles or unsweetened cocoa powder.

  4. Enjoy!
