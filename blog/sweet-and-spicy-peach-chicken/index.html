Sweet and Spicy Peach Chicken

Dinner Recipe

This dinner was soo easy but delicious! Sweet and spicy peach chicken with a side of spinach, edamame, sweet potato, honeydew melon, and goji berries. Plus extra sriracha cause I was craving spicy spicyyy all dang day. So yummy, I gotta make more of this!!Sweet and Spicy Peach ChickenServes: 1

  • 2 TBSP peach sweet potato jam (a no sugar Veggie Mama Brand natural jam)
  • 1/2 tsp sriracha
  • 4 oz raw chicken

Mix the jam and sriracha. Dip 4 oz raw chicken in the mixture. Put the chicken on a baking sheet. Bake at 350F for 25 minutes. Boom done. 2 ingredients, maxxXxXz flavor.
