"Chocolate Chip" Protein Waffle

Breakfast Quest Bar Recipe Recipe

Waffles all day waffles all night. This was my dessert last night! A chocolate chip protein waffle topped with a mint chocolate ice cream. The ice cream is the same recipe as my vanilla low calorie ice cream except I added 2 tsp unsweetened cocoa extract, an extra stevia packet, and 12-15 mint stevia drops. If you've never had waffles with ice cream, I seriously recommend it. I used to make waffles topped with soft serve ice cream all the time when I was a freshman living in dorms, and they were notttttt the healthy kind :x This is a healthy, EXTREMELY satisfying alternative to my long-lasting craving :DD"Chocolate Chip" Protein WaffleServes: 1

  • 1/2 cup old fashioned rolled oats
  • 1/2 scoop vanilla whey protein
  • 3 egg whites
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 stevia packet
  • 1/2 a chocolate brownie quest bar

First grind the oats into a flour. Then add the whey protein, egg whites, baking powder, vanilla extract, and stevia. Blend again until evenly mixed. Break up the quest bar into small pieces and swirl it into the batter. Spray a waffle maker with a nonstick spray and cook your waffle! If you don't have a waffle maker, the batter can also be used to make pancakes.
Nutritional info for the waffle without ice cream on top: 346 calories; 6g fat, 41 g carbs (14 g fiber), 38 g proteinNutritional info for the ice cream on top: 55 calories; 4 g fat, 5 g carbs (3 g fiber), 2 g protein
