Healthy Salted Walnut Fudge

Makes: 18 servings

  • 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder

  • 1/2 cup melted coconut oil

  • 1/2 cup peanut butter

  • 1/2 cup baking stevia

  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

  • sea salt, to taste

  1. Mix all ingredients in a medium bowl.

  2. Line a 4x9 dish or a bread loaf pan with saran wrap (for easy removal later), and pour in your fudge batter. Sprinkle more walnuts and salt on top.

  3. Freeze 45-60 mins, then cut into 18 fudgey squares.

  4. Enjoy! Store any extras in the freezer.
