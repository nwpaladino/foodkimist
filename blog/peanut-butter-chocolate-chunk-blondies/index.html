Peanut Butter Chocolate Chunk Blondies (vegan, gluten free)

Makes: 12 blondies

  • 1.5 cups rolled oats

  • 1/4 cup almond meal/flour

  • 1 TBSP ground flax

  • 1/4 cup baking stevia

  • 2 tsp baking powder

  • 3/4 cup unsweetened almond milk

  • 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce

  • 1/2 cup crunchy peanut butter (I use Crazy Richard's brand!)

  • 1 chocolate peanut butter square bar

  1. Grind the oats into a flour. Then in a medium bowl, mix the ground oats and the remaining ingredients (except for the square bar); mix very well until even.

  2. Chop up the square bar into small pieces, and fold into the batter.

  3. Spray a 9x9 baking dish with nonstick spray and pour your batter in. Bake at 350 F for 22-25 mins.

  4. Let cool 10 minutes then cut into 12 pieces. Enjoy!
