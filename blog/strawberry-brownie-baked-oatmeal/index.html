Strawberry Brownie Baked Oatmeal

Breakfast Recipe

Nothing less than extraordinary. Except for the..being asleep at 2 and waking up at 5:30 for work part. Meh.Strawberry Brownie Baked OatmealServes: 1

  • 1/2 cup old fashioned rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 2 TBSP pumpkin purée
  • 1.5 TBSP unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 egg white
  • 1 TBSP coconut flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 stevia packets
  • 1/4 cup chopped strawberries

Mix all of the ingredients except for the strawberries. Once well mixed and homogenous, lightly fold in 1/4 cup chopped strawberries (fresh or thawed from frozen). Spray a ramekin with nonstick spray and pour your oats in. Bake at 375 F for 30 mins.
To make the strawberry cream, mix 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt, 3-4 chopped strawberries, 1-2 TBSP water to desired consistency, and stevia to taste.
