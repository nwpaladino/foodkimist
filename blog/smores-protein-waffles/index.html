S'mores Protein Waffles

Breakfast Recipe

Breffis: Delicious s'mores protein waffles, a sweet 'cream cheese' icing, high protein cookie butter (Manbake brand), cocoa crunch square bar pieces, and cacao nibs. I swear I've seen infinite waffles on Instagram and in blogs lately; it's like my waffle maker missed me and devised a scheme to lure me back in. You win, waffles. I'll never neglect you again bb I swear <333S'mores Protein Waffles

  • 1/3 cup raw old fashioned rolled oats
  • 1/2 scoop s'mores whey protein (Cellucor brand)
  • 1 TBSP coconut flour
  • 1 TBSP unsweetened almond milk
  • 2 egg whites
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 stevia packet

Blend all of the waffle ingredients. Cook in a sprayed waffle maker and boom!!To make the sweet 'cream cheese' icing, mix 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt, 2 drops cream cheese flavor baking emulsion (from amazon. I also saw it at Walmart), 1 stevia packet, and water to desired consistency.
It goes perfectly with the cookie butter and chocolate square bar. The cookie butter has a cinnamon/graham flavor :D oh baby.
