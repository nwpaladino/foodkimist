Nut Butter Reviews (over 200!)

Review Tips

Soooo...... I've had a lot of nut butters in my dayy ;) I'm a bit of a hoarder when it comes to different flavors of things. I have this weird habit where I really like to try just a bit of every flavor possible, so my collection has built up. This applies to spices, mustards, salsas... and more. It's pretty bad XD because. I also have a habit of NOT using something when it's nearly gone. As if I could never buy more -_-. So I end up with a ton of a little of everything hahaha. So awful.
Anyways, I decided to keep a running list of all of the nut butters I have/had, as well as my opinion on them!! This may help you decide whether or not to purchase one you're interested in.
Anything in red and striked out is discontinued (as of last update: 03/20/2019). Anything in green is vegan! A lot of protein-fortified kinds I tried in the past have whey, but some of the vegan kinds are fortified with rice protein!Current StashBasse - Salted Caramel Cashew Butter: This nut butter is very creamy and has a thick, sweet flavor to it. Sometimes, though, I get bothered by the natural salted caramel flavor used to make this. Instead of using dairy to get the caramel flavor, they used "natural salted caramel flavor" and it has a pretty strong smell. Some may find this off-putting but some may find it delicious - my personal opinion on it changes daily. I will probably finish the jar just fine but not repurchase it since the flavor can be overwhelming.The Peanut Principle - Cereously Good (peanut butter with cocoa puffed rice): Another flavor for the dark chocolate lover. The base is very similar to the dark chocolate base of the smores flavor, but no marshmallow here. I am not a crunchy fan but I enjoy these tiny puffs!The Peanut Principle - Cereously Good (peanut butter with maple toast crisps): I don't get much maple flavor out of this.
