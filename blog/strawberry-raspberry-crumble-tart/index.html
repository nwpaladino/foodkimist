Strawberry + Raspberry Crumble Tart

Anyways, back to the bars. I took a faster, easier approach and made this strawberry + raspberry crumble tart. I like. Whenever there's a quest bar involved, you know it'll be good. Am I right or am I right?Strawberry + Raspberry Crumble TartServes: 1

  • 1 vanilla almond quest nutrition bar
  • 1/4 cup frozen raspberries
  • 1/4 cup frozen strawberries
  • 1 tsp stevia
  • 1 TBSP oat flour (ground up raw oats)
  • 1 TBSP raw oats
  • 1 TBSP unsweetened applesauce
  • 1/2 tsp stevia
  • a dash of cinnamon

Preheat the oven to 350 F. Microwave 1 vanilla almond quest bar for 8-10 seconds to soften. Spray a small ramekin with nonstick spray and flatten and press your bar inside to form a crust. Bake at 350 F for 8 minutes. Microwave the berries for 60 seconds. Mix and mash with 1 tsp stevia. Pour inside the baked crust. Mix the remaining ingredients. Sprinkle on top of the berries. There will be extra crumble sooo just eat dat. Bake another 5-6 mins at 350 F to crisp the top. Eat with a baby fork; it's mandatory.
