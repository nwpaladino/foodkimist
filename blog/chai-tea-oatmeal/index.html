Chai Tea Oatmeal

Serves: 1

  • 3/4 cup unsweetened almond milk

  • 2 bags of chai tea

  • 1/2 cup raw oats

  • 1 stevia packet

Microwave the almond milk for 90 seconds. Steep the chai tea in it (I used Tazo) for 3-4 mins, squeezing out all da juicyness.  Remove the tea bags, mix the milk, raw oats, and stevia packet. Microwave until all the liquid is cooked off, stirring often. Then eat!
