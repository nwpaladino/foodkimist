Caramel Apple Pie 1-Minute Cupcake

Quest Bar Recipe Recipe Sweets

Cupcake addict? Me? Guilty....Caramel Apple Pie 1-Minute CupcakeMakes: 2 cupcakes

  • 2 TBSP whole wheat pastry flour
  • 1 TBSP coconut flour
  • 1 TBSP unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 egg white
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp caramel extract
  • 1/4 tsp apple pie spice
  • 1 stevia packet
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 apple pie quest bar

Mix all of the listed ingredients except for the Quest Bar.  Either pour the batter into a sprayed mug to make one big cupcake or pour into two silicon cupcake molds to make two normal sized cupcakes. Then reak 1/4 apple pie quest bar into pieces and distribute amongst the two cupcakes. Microwave for 1 minute!I topped mine with a cinnamon raisin swirl icing (2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt, 1 tbsp cinnamon swirl whey protein by cellucor, 1 tbsp raisins, and 5-7 stevia drops).
