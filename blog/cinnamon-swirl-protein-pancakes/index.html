Cinnamon Swirl Protein Pancakes

Serves: 1

  • 1 scoop cinnamon swirl cellucor protein

  • 3/4 cup liquid egg whites

  • 2 tbsp coconut flour

  • 1/2 tsp baking powder

Just blend everything! Let sit 5 mins. Cook in a sprayed pan on medium until each side is lightly browned! Fluffy and yum.
