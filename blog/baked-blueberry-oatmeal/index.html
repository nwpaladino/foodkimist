Baked Blueberry Oatmeal

Serves: 1

  • 1/3 cup raw oats

  • 1/3 cup blueberries

  • 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk

  • 1 egg white

  • 1 TBSP coconut flour

  • 1/2 tsp baking powder

  • 1 stevia packet

Preheat the oven to 375 F. All ya gots ta do is mix all your ingredients in a bowl!! Pour in a ramekin sprayed with nonstick spray. Bake at 375 F for 25-30 mins! Enjoy!
