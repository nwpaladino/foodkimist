PB&J Protein Waffle

Serves: 1

  • 1/3 cup raw oats

  • 1/2 scoop peanut butter protein powder

  • 1 TBSP coconut flour

  • 2 egg whites

  • 1/2 tsp baking powder

  • 1 stevia packet

Grind the oats into a flour. Then add the remaining ingredients and blend again. Then cook in yo' waffley makerrrr!! (Spray first)

Make your icing and add the corresponding PB&J toppings!

Icing: Mix 1/2 scoop chocolate raspberry protein powder, 1 stevia packet, and 1-2 TBSP of water to desired consistency.

Toppings: Strawberries, 1/2 chopped up PB&J B Up protein bar, and sugar free chocolate pieces.
