Raspberry Vanilla Pancakes

Breakfast Recipe

MATH PROBLEM: If Kim leaves the gym travelling west at 45 mph, and her pancakes multiply vertically exponentially, how long does it take for her to completely devour the stack of pancakes? Now, thank you for attending another session of #pancakemath with Professor Me. I hope you do join us again for some more enrichment of your mind.

Vanilla Pancakes

Serves: 1

  • 1/4 cup coconut flour

  • 1/4 cup unsweetened almond milk

  • 4 egg whites

  • 1 TBSP plain greek yogurt

  • 1/2 tsp baking powder

  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

  • 1 stevia packet

Mix everything until no more lumps! Cook into 6 pancakes on a low heat for 4-5 mins, flip, and cook another 2 mins. Fluffy wuffy.

For the protein cream, I just mix peanut butter protein powder with unsweetened almond milk & a little stevia until it's a good consistency.

For the mashed raspberries, I just microwave raspberries and mash them with a little liquid stevia.
