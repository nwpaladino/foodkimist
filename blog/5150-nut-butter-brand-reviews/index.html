5150 Nut Butter Brand Reviews

Review Tips

I received 5150 nut butters recently to review! I'd like to preface my review by stating that if you're looking for a healthy nut butter, this is not the best choice for you. 3/4 of their nut butters contain added sugar; The Health Nut is all natural though. I have included a list of the ingredients at the end so that you can decide for yourself. If you're looking for an extremely delicious, mildly healthy nut butter, then this is definitely a brand for you!! :D To see my review on every nut butter I've owned (which is an overwhelming amount...) then go hereeee.5150 Nut Butter - Chocolate Biscuit:
Have you ever had one of those pirouette rolled wafers? Those long sticks that are filled with chocolate or cream? This reminds me sooo much of that dessert wafer! I love it so much! But it alsoooo reminds me of butterfingers! Kind of chocolate-y, kind of wafer-y. Creamy and runny, but also has a bit of grit to it. It's gooood. It's my favorite of the four flavors.5150 Nut Butter - Chocolate Chip Cookie:
Like the other flavors of this brand, it's creamy yet has some grit at the same time. It's hard to explain, but it's not bad! I wouldn't describe this flavor as chocolate chip cookie for some reason, but it's definitely cookie-ish.5150 Nut Butter - Cookies and Cream:
My second favorite of their flavors! It actually tastes just like oreos in a peanut butter form. Sooo good. Again, verrrry creamy.5150 Nut Butter - The Health Nut:
This is just peanut butter and almond butter blended, and it tastes just like that! Not bad, nothing special :pOverall, I am extremely pleased by this brand! Not a single disappointment by these flavors. And the texture; yesyesyesyes. You know I love me some creamy nut butter, and this is definitely creamy! So like I said, even though it's not the most healthy choice of nut butter, I would absolutely recommend this deliciousnesssss. Ingredients5150 Nut Butter - Chocolate Biscuit: Roasted Unsalted Peanuts, Sugar, Wheat Flour, Vegetable Oil (Palm and Hydrogenated Palm Oil), Milk Solids, Cocoa Butter, Chocolate Liquor, Partially Hydrolyzed Sugar Syrup, Caramel Color (From Wheat), Cocoa Powder, Soy Lecithin, Salt, Baking Soda, Polyglycerol Polyricinoleate, Artificial Flavoring, Red 40, Yellow 6, Blue 1, Yellow 5. ALLERGY WARNING: CONTAINS PEANUTS, WHEAT AND MILK. MAY CONTAIN TRACES OF TREE NUT, EGG, SESAME, AND SOY.5150 Nut Butter - Chocolate Chip Cookie: Roasted Unsalted Peanuts, Enriched Flour (Wheat Flour, Niacin, Reduced Iron, Thiamine Mononitrate, Riboflavin, Folic Acid), Semisweet Chocolate Chips (Sugar, Chocolate, Cocoa Butter, Dextrose, Soy Lecithin, Vanillin), Sugar, Vegetable Oil (Contains One or More of the Following: Canola Oil, Corn Oil, Palm Oil, Soybean Oil), High Fructose Corn Syrup, Leavening (Sodium Bicarbonate, Monocalcium Phosphate, Ammonium Bicarbonate), Salt, Natural and Artificial Flavors, Soy Lecithin (Emulsifier), Caramel Color ALLERGY WARNING: CONTAINS PEANUTS, WHEAT, AND SOY. MAY CONTAIN TRACES OF MILK, EGG, COCONUT, SESAME, TREE NUT AND PECANS.5150 Nut Butter - Cookies and Cream: Roasted Unsalted Peanuts, Dried Cane Syrup (Sugar), Enriched Wheat Flour (Flour, Niacin, Reduced Iron, Thiamine Mononitrate, Riboflavin, Folic Acid), Palm Oil, Expeller Pressed Soybean Oil, Cocoa Processed with Alkali, Tapioca Starch, Natural Vanilla Flavor, Natural Flavors, Cane Syrup, Baking Soda, Salt, Soy Lecithin, Vanilla Bean Seeds.
