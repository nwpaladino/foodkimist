Curry Apple Pie Turkey Meatloaf

Dinner Recipe

I bring to you... cousin of the Chili Pumpkin Turkey Meatloaf. The curry apple pie turkey meatloaf! Aka, what happens when you run out of pumpkin pie spice. This was a magical save for this week's meal prep. It came out SOOOO good! Stiff competition; I think it's my new favorite. I already know I've got to make another one for next week. Yummy yummy fun times.Curry Apple Pie Turkey MeatloafServes: 4-5

  • 1 lb lean ground turkey
  • 1/2 cup raw old fashioned rolled oats
  • 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce
  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 TBSP apple pie spice
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin

Mix everything together in a bowl using a utensil. Once evenly mixed, place in a bread loaf pan that's been sprayed to prevent sticking. Place in the oven at 350F and bake for 35 minutes, or until the meat is cooked completely. Slice and enjoy!I topped mine with tahini; it's the perfect addition. Top with whatever you prefer!
