Mint Chocolate Chip Protein Cookies

Quest Bar Recipe Recipe Sweets

Congrats. You get a cookie #party. I've been craving mint chocolate cookies so badly for weeks now so  I are sho happee.Mint Chocolate Chip Protein CookiesMakes: 12 cookies

  • 1 cup raw old fashioned rolled oats
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 scoop (36 g) mint chocolate chip whey protein (Cellucor brand)
  • 1/2 cup melted mint chocolate coconut butter (Sweet Spreads brand, or regular coconut butter)
  • 1 whole egg
  • 2 TBSP stevia
  • 2 TBSP unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 chocolate brownie quest nutrition protein bar (or double chocolate chunk flavor)

Grind the oats into a flour. Mix it in a bowl with the remaining ingredients (except for the quest bar). Then break apart 1 chocolate brownie quest nutrition protein bar into pieces and swirl it into a batter. Roll into 12 cookie dough balls and flatten on a sprayed baking sheet. Bake at 350 F for 7-8 mins!
