Strawberries and Cream 1-Minute Cupcake

Recipe Sweets

One of my favorites of my ongoing 1-minute cupcake recipe saga :DDStrawberries and Cream 1-Minute CupcakeMakes: 2 cupcakesCupcake Batter

  • 2 TBSP whole wheat pastry flour
  • 1 TBSP coconut flour
  • 1 TBSP unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 egg white
  • 1/2 tsp strawberry extract
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 8-10 vanilla stevia drops
  • 2 stevia packets
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder

Icing

  • 1 TBSP plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 TBSP vanilla whey protein
  • 5-7 vanilla stevia drops
  • 1 mashed strawberry

Mix all of the listed cupcake batter ingredients.  Either pour the batter into a sprayed mug to make one big cupcake or pour into two silicon cupcake molds to make two normal sized cupcakes. I prefer making 2 cupcakes. Microwave for 1 minute!
To  make the icing, mix the plain greek yogurt, whey protein, and stevia. Then stir in 1 strawberry that has been mashed. Top your cupcakes!
