Salted Caramel Protein Cookies

Makes: 10 cookies

  • 1 cup ground up raw oats (80 g)

  • 2 scoops salted caramel quest nutrition protein powder

  • 2 stevia packets

  • 1/2 tsp baking soda

  • 1 whole egg

  • 1 TBSP coconut oil

  • 1/2 tsp imitation caramel extract (Optional)

  • 6-7 tbsp unsweetened almond milk

  • 1 chocolate caramel Simple bar (Detour Bar brand)

In a bowl, mix everything except for the detour bar!! Mixmixmix it rull good. Roll the dough into 10 cookie dough balls, placing a slice of a chocolate caramel simple bar in the center of each. Bake at 350 F for 9 minutes. EEEEEAT. 

Nutritional info for 1 cookie: 85 cals; 2.6 g fat, 8.8 g carbs (1.6 g fiber), 7.5 g protein
