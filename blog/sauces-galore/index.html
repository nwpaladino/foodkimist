Sauces Galore!

Featured Posts Recipe Snacks

I was told by someone that they noticed that I "enjoy saucy foods." It kind of made me stop and ask..... what? No I don't? Do I? Then I looked at my page and realized how much I top my meals with random mixed sauces! It's easy to cater to cravings by just throwing a ton of spices and things together and putting it on your meat or veggies :) I just listen to my cravings and make it happen haha.
There's a few I've really liked and then make again, but eventually I forget about them because I find a new favorite! So I've created a list of my favorites (partially for myself :p) but also so that you all can try them!
