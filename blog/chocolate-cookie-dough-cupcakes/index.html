Chocolate Cookie Dough Cupcakes

Makes: 3 cupcakes

  • 1/2 scoop molten chocolate Cellucor protein

  • 1/2 cup raw oats

  • 2/3 cup unsweetened almond milk

  • 1 TBSP cocoa powder

  • 1 TBSP coconut flour

  • 1 TBSP ground flax seeds

  • 1/2 tsp baking powder

  • 2 stevia packets

Blend all of your ingredients in a blender! It helps to grind the oats down first. Once even, pour your batter into 3 cupcake molds sprayed with nonstick spray. Bake at 350 for 20 mins. Let cool and enjoy!!

Go-To Cookie Dough

  • 3 TBSP oat flour (old fashioned rolled oats ground up)

  • 1 TBSP coconut flour

  • 1 TBSP unsweetened applesauce

  • 1 TBSP sugar free breakfast syrup

  • 2 TBSP water

  • 10-12 drops Vanilla creme sweetleafstevia drops

  • 1/4 tsp imitation butter extract

Just mix everything in a bowl!!
