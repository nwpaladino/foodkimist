Cookie Dough Stuffed Pancake Puffs

Makes: 6-7

  • 1/3 cup raw old fashioned oats

  • 1/2 scoop cookie dough protein powder

  • 1 TBSP coconut flour

  • 2-3 TBSP water

  • 2 egg whites

  • 1/2 tsp baking powder

  • 1 stevia packet

  • 1 cookie dough Quest bar

  • 1/2 bar of sugar free chocolate

  1. Grind your oats into a flour. Then add the remaining ingredients except for the quest bar and chocolate. Blend again. This is your batter.

  2. Cut 1 cookie dough Quest bar into 8 pieces. Except you only need 7 so EAT DAT extra.

  3. Begin to heat an ebelskiver pan. If you don’t have one, you can make these like pancakes. Spray the heated ebelskiver pan with a nonstick spray, fill a position 1/2 way with the pancake batter, place a piece of quest bar piece inside and a piece of the chocolate. Then cover it with more batter. Repeat for all 7 positions on the pan.

  4. Once the bottoms are lightly brown, flip to cook the other side. Once lightly browned on the second side, remove!
