Oreo Banana Ice Cream

  • 3-4 frozen bananas

  • 1/2 scoop (15 g) Cookies & Cream Pea Protein

  • Toppings! (oreos, cacao nibs, chocolate stevia syrup)

  1. In a food processor or high speed blender, add your frozen bananas and flavored pea protein (I got this from truenutrition.com). If you don't have the protein, add a crushed oreo instead.

  2. Blend until smooth! It will be thick and creamy.

  3. Add your toppings! Oreos are not gluten free, so if you are gluten free, use an alternative "oreo" brand like this.
