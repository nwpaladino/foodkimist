Chocolate Orange Dream Protein Donuts

Quest Bar Recipe Recipe Sweets

Donut time? Always and forever. Breakfast was chocolate orange dream protein donuts. Still in bliss from the previous cookies and cream protein donuts; wanted more today :DChocolate Orange Dream Protein DonutsMakes: 4Donut Base

  • 1/3 cup raw old fashioned rolled oats
  • 1 TBSP almond meal/flour
  • 1/2 scoop vanilla whey protein
  • 3 egg whites
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 30-40 orange stevia drops
  • 1 stevia packet
  • 1 double chocolate chunk quest bar

Orange Creamsicle Icing

  • 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 TBSP orange creamsicle whey protein (from truenutrition.com)
  • 1 stevia packet

Preheat the oven to 350 F. Grind the oats into a flour. Then add the remaining donut batter ingredients (except for the quest bar) and blend again. Break apart 1 double chocolate chunk quest bar and swirl it into the batter. Spray a donut mold pan with nonstick spray. Pour batter amongst 4 donut molds. Bake at 350 F for 10 minutes.
For the icing, mix the three listed ingredients. Place icing on the donuts.
Nutritional info for 1 donut without icing: 103 calories; 3 g fat, 12 g carbs (5 g fiber), and 12 g protein
