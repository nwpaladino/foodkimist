FAQs

When will my order arrive?

Shipments go out Monday-Friday. Generally, you can expect your order to arrive 1-2 days after the next available shipping day.

How long do the macarons stay fresh?

Upon delivery, your macarons will remain fresh in the fridge up to 7 days and in the freezer up to 2 months. You should bring them to room temperature before consuming.

Do you deliver or do pickups?

No delivery or pickups, but free shipping is available within Georgia.

Do you do events?

Unfortunately I no longer support custom events, but I ship the currently available macarons anywhere within Georgia. I also supply event display towers (4-tier or 10-tier) to help create a beautiful display for your event. You can find everything in the store here. Please use code TWOPLUS for 10% off your order.

What are the gift box options?

If you’re planning on gifting a box of macarons, feel free to leave a personalized gift note during checkout to be included with delivery.

Can I customize a macaron order?

You may select your preferred flavors from the flavor list when you go to checkout. You cannot customize the colors since macaron selection is based on current availability.

What are the macaron flavors?

Flavors change often and are available for a limited time. Check out the macaron store for current availability.

Are there any vegan, dairy-free, and/or gluten-free macarons?

All of our macarons are vegan and dairy-free by design. If you would like gluten-free macarons, please specify that upon ordering so we can help you select the appropriate flavors. See our ingredients guide for more information.
